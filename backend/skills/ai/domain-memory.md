# AI Due Diligence Domain Knowledge

## AI Due Diligence Four-Pillar Framework

Cover four dimensions in every AI company evaluation:
1. **Product & Commercialization** — Does the product solve a real problem? Will customers pay?
2. **Technical Moat** — Is the technology genuinely differentiated, or will it commoditize?
3. **Scale & Growth** — Is the company growing sustainably? What are the unit economics?
4. **Risk & Compliance** — What could go wrong? Regulatory, technical, market, and geopolitical risks.

## AI Risk Severity Reference

Rate each identified risk as High, Medium, or Low using these criteria:
- **Regulatory pressure** — Is there active enforcement or pending legislation directly targeting this area?
- **Commercial dependency** — If this risk materializes, what % of revenue or customer base is affected?
- **Substitutability** — Can customers easily switch to an alternative if the risk event occurs?

## Common AI Company Red Flags

- Revenue concentrated in 1–2 customers (>50%)
- Training data of unclear provenance or known copyright issues
- Single cloud provider dependency without a migration plan
- No published model evaluations or third-party benchmarks
- GPU supply concentrated in a single vendor with no diversification plan
- Key technical personnel without equity vesting or retention incentives
- Operating in a jurisdiction with active AI regulatory enforcement without local counsel
