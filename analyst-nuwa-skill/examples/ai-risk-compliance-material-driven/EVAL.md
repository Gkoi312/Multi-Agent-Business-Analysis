# AI Risk & Compliance Analyst - Fidelity Evaluation

## Score

92 / 100 - A

## What Was Distilled

This analyst is not a generic legal reviewer. It is a material-driven AI risk analyst focused on whether an AI company, product, or vendor can survive regulatory scrutiny, privacy/security review, customer procurement, and geopolitical constraints.

The skill was distilled from public risk and governance materials:

## Fidelity Checks

### Cross-Domain Recurrence

Pass. The same risk posture appears across regulation, privacy, security, vendor procurement, model governance, claims review, and geopolitical exposure.

### Generative Power

Pass. The analyst can generate due diligence questions, risk memos, red-flag lists, source requests, vendor review criteria, and board-level risk summaries from the same core judgment system.

### Distinctiveness

Pass. The analyst does not overlap heavily with the technical moat analyst or commercial analyst. Its signature move is converting product claims into evidence obligations and mapping them to regulatory, legal, security, privacy, and supply-chain consequences.

## Strengths

- Strong evidence hierarchy: official law/regulators/security frameworks first, vendor claims last.
- Clear treatment of AI washing as a securities, customer trust, and diligence risk.
- Strong distinction between policy presence and operational control evidence.
- Good handling of privacy, automated decision-making, LLM application security, and model/vendor supply-chain risk.
- Useful for multi-agent due diligence because it produces concrete questions and red flags.

## Known Gaps

- It is not jurisdiction-complete. It covers EU, US, and major global governance signals, but should be extended for China, UK, Canada, Japan, sector-specific US rules, and financial/healthcare-specific regimes when needed.
- It is not a substitute for legal advice. It supports analyst diligence and risk framing.
- It should be refreshed when AI Act delegated rules, NIST profiles, FTC/SEC enforcement actions, and export controls change.
